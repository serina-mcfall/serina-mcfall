# Hi — I'm Serina

Software Developer in Auckland. AuDHD mum, career-pivot at 45. Accessibility-first.

## Why I'm here

I worked in customer service for nearly 30 years, from the supermarket I
started at 16 to the short-holiday company I left at 45. Three decades on
the other side of the counter taught me that respect is earned, not given.
That strength is built. That the people with a fighting spirit often find
their path. And that I'm stronger than I thought.

I went back to school at 45 because I finally believed I could. Dev Academy
turned out to be the road I'd been looking for. I've found it, and I intend
to be on this journey for the long haul.

I want my work to serve people who get left out. **Brightly Wired** is a
community platform I'm co-building because I wish I'd had something like it
growing up — and at three, when my son's school called him "naughty, a
bully, difficult to have in class," and again later when I was being
diagnosed myself. The same idea drives **bright-fit** (an ND-friendly
employer directory + application tracker for the adults still hiding who
they are) and the **Learning Companion Project** (an AI study companion
shaped for ND brains). Three projects, one audience: the unheard child,
the struggling mother, the adult afraid that society will judge them.

I also want my kids to watch me do this. They are neurodivergent too. I
want them to see a mother who stepped out of her comfort zone, took an
unknown road full of curves and blockers, and kept walking it. I want them
to know they can carve out their own path and create bright futures for
themselves — and that being who they are is enough.

What keeps me coming back to the keyboard? My brain is part chaos, part
calm. The calm has finally found its foundation on the backend — schemas,
migrations, seeds, the relational layer I love. It took me 45 years to
find the place that makes my heart sing and my mind a calm ocean. Now that
I've found it, I want to keep exploring everything coding and the tech
industry has to offer. That's why I've applied to continue at Dev Academy's
**AI Launchpad** (starting 27 July), and why I'm aiming at **Summer of
Tech** after.
